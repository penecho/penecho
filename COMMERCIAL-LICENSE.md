## Counting users and upgrading

Monthly active users (MAU) means distinct people who access or use PenEcho features in the named product during a calendar month, including anonymous users. Count each person once across those features; do not count the product's unrelated users, total registrations, or company headcount. Use reasonable, consistent deduplication and retain aggregate counts; no personal user data needs to be sent to PenEcho.

If the $1,000 plan exceeds 10,000 MAU for two consecutive calendar months, contact PenEcho within 30 days after the second month to upgrade. The upgrade charge is the $9,000 annual price difference multiplied by the fraction of the original license year remaining at the end of the second month. The original expiry date stays unchanged. The lower plan remains valid during this 30-day upgrade period. Renew at the tier appropriate to the current usage.
